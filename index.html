Our story

A small bakery with a big heart.

Arangan Bakery started with a simple idea: the neighbourhood deserves bread that was baked this morning, not last week. We knead, proof and bake in small batches so every bun is soft, every cookie is crisp and every cake tastes like a celebration.

Today we are a cosy stop for families, students and office-goers alike — somewhere to grab a quick chai, share a plate of pani puri, or pick up a cake for someone special.

  • Baked at sunrise

    Fresh trays every morning, no day-old shortcuts.

  • Real filter coffee

  • Made with care
